Privacy Policy

Trippi · Effective October 25, 2026

Trippi is a packing list app. It is built so that what you put into it stays on your phone. This policy explains what the app stores, what little it sends, and how you can turn that off.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Trippi is stored only on your device:

  • Your packing lists, items, categories, notes, destinations and travel dates
  • Photos you take or choose for your items, including any background removal, which is done on the device
  • Your profile details, if you fill them in (name, email, home city)
  • Your settings

We do not have accounts or servers, and we cannot see any of this. It may be included in your device's own backups (such as iCloud Backup), which are controlled by you and Apple.

Camera and photos

Trippi asks for camera access only when you choose to photograph an item. When you pick a photo from your library, you choose which photo to share, and Trippi sees only that one. Photos are never uploaded.

Anonymous usage data and crash reports

To understand which features are used and to fix bugs, Trippi uses two Google services, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They receive:

  • Usage events, such as "a list was created", "an item was checked" or "a setting was changed", with simple values like counts, the number of days until a trip, or the destination country you picked
  • Crash reports: technical details about what went wrong when the app crashes
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate region
  • A random app identifier created by Firebase, which is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID

Trippi never sends anything you type, such as list titles, item names, notes or your profile, and never sends your photos.

This data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. Trippi does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google processes this data under its own Firebase privacy terms.

Turning it off

You can stop all usage data and crash reports at any time: open Trippi, go to Settings and turn off Share usage data.

Deleting your data

Deleting an item, a list, or the Trippi app removes that data from your device. Because the usage data we receive is anonymous, we cannot look it up by your name or email, but you are welcome to contact us with any request.

Children

Trippi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
